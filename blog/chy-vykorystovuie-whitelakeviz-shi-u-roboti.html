    Does WhiteLakeViz Use AI in Our Work?

    The short answer is: yes, but very carefully and selectively.

    Artificial intelligence has become an undeniable part of the modern creative industry, yet we view it not as a replacement for traditional 3D graphics, but strictly as an auxiliary tool at the very beginning of a project.

    When we turn to AI:

    • Rapid concepts and ideation. When we need to test the mood of a scene, explore color palettes, or show a client a preliminary direction within minutes—before full-scale production begins.
    • Draft concept visualization. For quickly validating hypotheses during the briefing stage or discussing references, ensuring that we and the client share the exact same vision.

    Why a classic 3D pipeline remains the core of our work:

    Generative neural networks excel at abstract ideas, but in real-world architectural and product visualization, accuracy, control, and predictability are non-negotiable. That is why 95% of our workflow relies on classic, hands-on craftsmanship:

    • Precision 3D modeling built strictly from real blueprints, dimensions, and specifications.
    • Physically based materials (PBR) calibrated to react accurately to light from any camera angle.
    • Custom lighting and final rendering, where every shadow, reflection, and surface texture is under the artist's complete control.

    For us, AI is a fast sketchbook for early ideas—while true quality, photorealism, and attention to detail are crafted by hand.
